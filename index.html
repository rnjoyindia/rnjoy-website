01 / Company

Building a modern
Indian fashion business.

R7 LIFESTYLE PRIVATE LIMITED is a growing fashion and lifestyle company focused on creating modern, affordable and quality-driven products for today’s Indian consumer.

The company operates with a strong focus on men’s fashion, combining contemporary design, reliable quality and accessible pricing.

With an integrated approach covering product development, manufacturing, sourcing, retail and digital channels, the company is building a scalable fashion business with long-term growth potential.
